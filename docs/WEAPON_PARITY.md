# JavaScript weapon reference and native contracts

Reference: `flynncrochon/Snake-Survivors`, commit
`b6cb72e66eebc1291d57efe63ebbe93ec602e863`, locally available as
`../web_snake/src/survivors` and `../web_snake/src/core/battle_royale_app.js`.
`game_tests/fixtures/javascript_weapon_oracle.json` was generated by executing
the original JavaScript getters and module constants in a browser. The Python
matrix reads that independent fixture. Base weapon formulas retain this source
reference. Evolved weapons also apply the intentional native upgrade tuning
described below; the original fixture is preserved rather than rewritten to
match the implementation. The C++ contracts exercise the simulation without
raylib or a display.

The native unit conversion is **one original cell = eight simulation pixels**.
An enemy or summon grid step always traverses one cardinal cell. Projectile
positions can remain fractional in simulation; the renderer rasterizes them to
the same integer pixel grid as every other sprite.

## Native evolution upgrade tuning

The native game intentionally strengthens evolutions following the requested
balance pass. All base rank formulas above remain unchanged. Evolving still
replaces its family with its distinct orbit, projectile, well, brood, aura,
beam, laser, or grapple mechanic; it does not run both versions together.

`data/evolutions.csv` authors positive multipliers for evolved damage, cast
cooldown, effect area, and targeting range. `range` is optional and defaults to
1 for older content. The shipped values in that file are the current tuning
source. The loader rejects non-positive multipliers. Related damage and area
effects read the configured values. Range scales specification-based target
acquisition; orbit geometry and Ancient's growing laser range retain their
separate formulas below.

Replacing a weapon also retains offensive investment that the base could use.
Let `A=1+Volley`, `C=Cooldown`, `T=Duration`, and
`N=max(0,floor((T−1)/.25+.0001))`, using the resolved player stats including
character bonuses. After the authored damage multiplier, the following
additional factors apply before eligible direct-damage bonuses and rounding:

| Evolution | Additional damage factor | Retained behavior |
|---|---|---|
| Ouroboros | `A/C` | Keeps 44 fangs and the per-victim hit interval; extra volleys and faster base firing increase damage without adding orbit actors. |
| Serpent's Gatling | None | Volley still adds projectiles and Cooldown still shortens its burst interval. |
| Singularity Mortar | `A` | Keeps one bomb per cast; the bomb carries the damage investment from additional volleys. Cast cooldown and well Duration still scale normally. |
| Hydra Brood | `A` | Keeps one six-parent egg per cast; parent and child bites carry the Volley investment. Cast cooldown and rounded summon lifetimes still scale normally. |
| Miasma | `A×(1+.5N)/C` | Concentrates volleys, Duration aftershocks, and attack speed into the persistent aura's half-second ticks. |
| Consumption Beam | `T/C` | Retains the Duration and attack-speed investment that allowed overlapping base beams. Volley still adds persistent beams. |
| Shatter Fang | None | Volley, cast cooldown, projectile Duration, critical hits, and bounded splinter generations retain their individual effects. |
| Ancient Brood Pit | `1/C` | Preserves attack-speed investment in laser damage while maintaining its bounded persistent laser population. |
| Serpent's Reckoning | None | Volley still adds grapples and Cooldown shortens its cast interval. |

The concentrated factors retain support bonuses without multiplying persistent
actor counts or increasing tick frequency. Existing family and actor caps remain
in force. These factors describe damage resolution, not a promise that every
individual hit or random fight always exceeds its unevolved counterpart.

Additional native actor changes:

- Eight of Ouroboros's 44 fangs orbit at approximately 1–3 cells, with reduced
  radial wobble; the other 36 retain the outer orbit. Area scales their orbit
  positions. Contact damage and victim cooldown use the current resolved spec;
  the hit radius respects the authored evolution area multiplier without
  gaining an extra Toxic Expanse radius multiplier.
- Singularity's lob lands after 0.65 seconds and the well hits immediately on
  landing; subsequent well ticks retain the 0.4-second interval. Its detonation
  deals `6.25×` its current unrounded tick damage,
  rounded before a possible critical hit. Authored damage and concentrated
  Volley investment therefore affect the blast as well as the well ticks.
- Hydra child bites are `0.75×` the parent's resolved hatch damage. Eggs refresh
  both parent and child damage at hatch; an already hatched snake retains its
  damage snapshot. Indirect summon bites continue to exclude direct-damage
  bonuses and critical hits.
- Miasma's breathing radius uses the resolved authored area instead of a
  hardcoded eight-cell radius. Its tick remains every half second.
- Consumption's shipped range multiplier is 1.2, giving a 12-cell targeting
  range before Serpent's Reach, rather than the original ten cells.
- Ancient lasers deal the current resolved damage times `1+.25g` and the
  stored overflow multiplier. Their half-width is the resolved initial width
  times `1+.16g`, with Toxic Expanse applied. After growth reaches 10, each later
  seed adds `.15×(6+floor((rank−1)/3))×A` to the stored overflow multiplier;
  it creates no additional turret or laser actor.
- Reckoning's drag deals `0.625×` its current unrounded damage and the bite
  blast deals `1.25×`, with their usual critical-hit rounding. Drag width uses
  its resolved area with Toxic Expanse; the bite radius is twice that width.
  Devouring still removes the captured victim's remaining HP and grants
  growth/XP rather than healing.

Native balance assertions must compare max-rank base and evolved weapons with
the same passives, seed, enemy schedule, player route, and duration. Interpret
damage alongside kills, setup time, target availability, and utility. The
balance report records measured scenarios and limits; source-oracle checks
retain independent JavaScript values plus the separately authored native
tuning rather than deriving expected damage from the native getter.

## Shared bonuses and intentional native fixes

- Rapid Fire cooldown multiplier: `.85^rank`.
- Dead Eye: `.125×rank` crit chance, maximum 100%; crit doubles damage.
- Ravenous Maw: direct damage rises linearly from +10% at rank 1 to +50% at
  rank 8; XP multiplier is `1+.25×rank`. Indirect poison and nest bites retain
  their original exclusions.
- Chronofield: `1+.25×rank`; Toxic Expanse: `1+.15×rank`; Serpent's Reach:
  `1+.12×rank`. Projectile hit radii remain fixed when the JS only scales
  search, orbit, cone or area radius.
- Serpent Scales is a replenishing shield, with charge counts
  `[1,1,1,2,2,3,3,3]` and cooldown linearly decreasing from 10 to 4 seconds.
  It does not grant flat armor.

The JavaScript declares a .16-second grid pull interval and pull strength for
Singularity but never uses them. The native port restores the advertised pull.
The original applies fractional bullet/lash knockback; native displacements
preserve cardinal grid bodies to prevent turn pixels and broken trails.
Combat displacement is animated through a cosmetic presentation offset; its
logical distance, hit timing, slow and damage remain unchanged.
Seeded simulation RNG replaces `Math.random()`/wall-clock dependence so bugs
can be reproduced. The native enemy director, 1,800-enemy limit, player HP,
fixed-step timing and character framework remain native game systems; these
weapon contracts are not a claim of identical full-run RNG, spawn pacing, or UI.

Collision uses swept projectiles, stable enemy IDs and per-projectile hit
histories. Actor pools and visual effects are capped; damage and forced moves
are staged so moving an enemy cannot invalidate a spatial-grid traversal.
Consumption stores death notifications before dead-enemy compaction, preventing
lost absorption when another weapon delivers the final blow.

Damage modifiers, raw projectile damage and enemy HP use IEEE doubles, matching
JavaScript numbers. Each weapon retains its original rounding stage: Sidewinder
and the leviathan round before doubling a critical hit; direct fangs and lash
round their final critical damage. Cobra spit keeps fractional damage and samples
its critical hit when fired. Regression cases include `round(90×1.15)=103`,
`round(450×1.15)=518`, and `round(150×2×1.15)=345`.

Live upgrades reuse one cached spec per equipped family each frame. In-flight
gun/fang/ricochet impacts and active beam/poison/Miasma ticks read current rank
and damage bonuses. Delayed Nova aftershocks calculate current raw damage before
halving and rounding. Egg brood count and bite damage are read at hatch; hatched
bite damage stays fixed. Nest/Hydra Duration upgrades change the total allowed
lifespan without resetting summon age. For example one Chronofield rank allows
88 parent ticks (`round(70×1.25)`), or 7.04 seconds, rather than exactly 7 seconds.
Cobra pits read current duration and spawn limit, living cobras read current spit
damage/cadence, and spit already in flight retains its launch damage/critical
roll. Existing cobra and base-beam lifespan remain their spawn/cast snapshots.
Rank and cooldown upgrades preserve elapsed attack time; an evolution chest
clears the replaced family's actors. The test harness may remove equipment to
stop new casts while observing old effects; those actors retain their last
configured values as a deliberate testing fallback.
